### The quadratic formula

The roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$ are given by

$$
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}
$$

When the discriminant $\Delta = b^2 - 4ac$ is negative, the roots are complex.

<details>
<summary>Derivation</summary>

Complete the square: $ax^2 + bx = -c$ becomes $(x + \frac{b}{2a})^2 = \frac{b^2 - 4ac}{4a^2}$.

</details>
